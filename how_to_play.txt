           NYMPHIAD — HOW TO PLAY

GOAL
----
Each level has a glowing coin. Reach it
to advance to the next level. Beat all
8 levels to win!

Your time is recorded from level 1.
Deaths pause the clock - only active
play time counts. Try for a fast time!


CONTROLS
--------
Move        A / D   or   Left / Right
Jump        W       or   Up
Grab box    Hold X near a box (to push or pull)
Restart     R


HAZARDS
-------
Spikes    - Triggered when you get close.
            Back away to make them retract.

Crusher   - Drops from the ceiling when
            you walk beneath it. Keep moving!


MECHANICS
---------
Wrapping  - Walk off one edge of the screen
            and you'll appear on the other side.
            This applies to boxes too!

Switches  - Arrow icons on the wall. Walk into
            one to shift the camera and reveal
            a new area of the level.

Boxes     - Hold X near a box to grab and drag
            it. Use boxes to reach higher ground
            or block hazards.


TIPS
----
- You can carry a box through a screen wrap.
- Spikes retract if you move away in time.
- The crusher returns to the ceiling after
  a short delay - wait it out if needed.
- Dying doesn't reset your level progress,
  only the current room resets.

                Good luck!
